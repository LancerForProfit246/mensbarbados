Who We Are

M.E.N.S (Men Empowerment Network Support) is a registered charity in Barbados representing more than 250 men committed to addressing issues affecting men and boys while driving empowerment across communities.

We create essential safe spaces for men and boys to emotionally “offload” and confront the social, psychological, and emotional issues that affect their well-being. Our work is grounded in research and built to respond to an evolving psychosocial landscape.

We empower men at the individual, group, and policy levels through practical programmes, mentorship, psychosocial support, and community engagement.

The SEA Approach

Support

Safe spaces, counselling pathways, mentoring, and practical social support that help men and boys process challenges in healthy ways.

Education

Programmes that build emotional intelligence, parenting capacity, life skills, employability, and personal development.

Advocacy

Community and policy-level engagement focused on improving outcomes for men, boys, and families across Barbados.

Our Mission

M.E.N.S is dedicated to empowering men and boys with agility, relevance, and compassion. We help them grow through challenge, build emotional awareness, and strengthen accountability to family and community.

Our programmes are centered on mental health, adolescent male development, psychosocial support, parenting education, mentorship, emotional intelligence, contemporary gender issues, and men’s business development.

Programme Highlights

SAFE Zone

A 10-week adolescent boys programme for ages 12–18 focused on life skills, emotional intelligence, mentorship, self-awareness, and positive decision-making.

Bridge to the World

A six-month development programme helping young men build employability, entrepreneurship, confidence, and career readiness after exiting school.

Men Leh We Reason

A platform for honest conversation, reflection, and public engagement around the real issues affecting men, families, and communities.

M.E.N.S in Action

Schools Outreach & Adolescent Support

Schools across Barbados have called for stronger intervention and mentorship for boys. M.E.N.S responds by entering schools and creating spaces where boys can receive male guidance, support, and constructive engagement.

Our outreach responds directly to issues such as deviance, anxiety, violence, emotional suppression, and the need for healthy role models.

MENS Talk is our flagship monthly support space for adult men.
Parenting Empowerment provides guidance, tools, and real support for families.
